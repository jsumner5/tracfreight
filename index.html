Why shippers go direct

Most freight changes hands two or three times before it reaches a truck. Working with the carrier directly cuts out the middle — and the markup, the hold music, and the finger-pointing when something changes.

  • No broker markup — your rate is a carrier rate, start to finish.
  • Real updates — status comes from the source, not relayed through three inboxes.
