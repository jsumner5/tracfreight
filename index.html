What we haul

Three ways to move freight with us — pick the one that fits your dock, your load, and your schedule.

SpecialtyDrop Trailer Service

We spot a trailer at your facility and your team loads on your schedule — no driver waiting, no detention clock. When it's ready, we swap or pick up and haul. Built for shippers who load around the clock or need a buffer at the dock.

SpecialtyFlatbed

Building materials, equipment, steel, and oversized or awkward freight that doesn't belong in a box. Our flatbed capacity runs Georgia and Tennessee daily, with interstate reach when the lane calls for it.

Core53′ Dry Van

Palletized general freight in clean, road-ready 53-foot dry vans. Local Georgia shuttles, regional lanes, and dedicated repeat runs — the workhorse service this company was built on.
